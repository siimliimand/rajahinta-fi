## 3. English Outreach Email Template

**Subject:** Request for Proposal: Written Legal Opinion (Finnish Alcohol Act / Consumer Law / Rajahinta.fi)

```text
Dear Counsel,

We are requesting a proposal for a formal written legal opinion concerning a new cross-border beverage cost-comparison service, Rajahinta.fi, targeted at Finnish consumers.

BRIEF OVERVIEW:
Rajahinta.fi is an independent landed-cost calculator and price index. It provides consumers with estimated total costs for purchasing beverages abroad (e.g. Systembolaget in Sweden) and importing them to Finland: foreign retail price, shipping, estimated Finnish alcohol excise duty, and beverage-container duty.

The platform is NOT a merchant or intermediary:
- No checkout, no order taking, and no payment processing for alcohol.
- No shipping or transport arrangements.
- The only monetization is a software subscription (Free / Premium €4.99/mo for export/declaration tooling).
- Outbound merchant links contain no affiliate IDs, no purchase tracking, and no commissions.

SCOPE OF INQUIRY:
We require a formal written opinion assessing the service under Finnish law across 12 specific topics:
1. Alcohol Act marketing rules (Alkoholilaki 1102/2017)
2. Price-list and price-information provisions
3. Hyperlinks to foreign alcohol retailers
4. Comparative advertising regulations
5. Search-engine indexing & crawlability
6. Software subscription monetization
7. Email notifications policy
8. Personalization and privacy
9. Objective, deterministic product rankings (strictly no paid placement)
10. Treatment of strong vs. mild alcoholic beverages
11. User-generated content boundaries
12. Age-gating mechanism (self-attestation vs. strong eID)

ATTACHMENT:
A comprehensive briefing package detailing each topic with verbatim UI copy, data flow, and code references is provided in `docs/legal-briefing-package.md`.

PROPOSAL REQUEST:
Please provide:
1. Fee estimate (fixed fee or capped hourly arrangement).
2. Estimated turnaround time.
3. Lead counsel credentials in Finnish alcohol regulatory law and e-commerce.

Kind regards,
Siim Liimand
Rajahinta.fi
```
